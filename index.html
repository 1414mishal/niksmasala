The Niks Promise

Crafted honestly, every single pack.

Three commitments behind every Niks Masala blend.

Farm-direct sourcing

We buy spices directly from growers across Karnataka, Kerala & Andhra — no middlemen, no adulteration.

Small-batch roasting

Every blend is slow-roasted in small batches at our FSSAI-certified facility — never machine-extruded.

No fillers, no shortcuts

Zero artificial colours, preservatives or anti-caking agents. Just spice. Read the label.

Bulk & wholesale

For restaurants, retailers & gift programmes.

We supply restaurants and cloud kitchens across the country, retail stores, and corporate gifting programmes. Custom packaging available on larger orders. Talk to us directly — no forms, no waiting.
