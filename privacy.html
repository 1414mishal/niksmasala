This Privacy Policy describes how Iniha Exports Pvt Ltd (operating as "Niks Masala", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you visit or buy from this website. We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, password (stored hashed), security question and answer.
  • Order data: shipping address, items ordered, payment method, payment status, order notes.
  • Communication data: messages you send via the contact form, newsletter subscriptions.
  • Technical data: browser type, device, approximate location (from IP), pages visited. We do not currently run third-party analytics.

2. Why we collect it

  • To fulfil and deliver your order, including handing your shipping details to our courier partners.
  • To process payment via Razorpay (a PCI-DSS compliant gateway). We never store your card or UPI credentials.
  • To send transactional updates (order confirmation, shipment, delivery).
  • To respond to customer service queries.
  • To comply with tax and accounting obligations (GST invoicing, audit).

3. Who we share it with

We share the minimum data necessary with:

  • Razorpay Software Pvt Ltd — payment processing.
  • Courier partners (Delhivery, Shiprocket, India Post, etc.) — shipping fulfilment.
  • Supabase Inc. — database hosting (EU/US region).
  • Statutory authorities — only when legally required (court order, tax notice, etc.).

We never sell your data to third-party marketers.

4. Data retention

Order records are retained for 8 years to satisfy Indian tax and GST audit requirements. Account data is retained until you request deletion. Contact form messages are retained for 12 months.

5. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") for non-essential data.
  • Right to withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Right to nominate another person to exercise rights on your behalf.
  • Right to grievance redressal.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@niksmasala.com.

6. Cookies

We use only first-party cookies and browser localStorage to remember your cart, login session and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers at this time.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes. Payments use Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 environment. Database access is restricted via row-level security. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password and keep your account credentials confidential.

8. Children

This site is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be flagged on the home page for 30 days.
