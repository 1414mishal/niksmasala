Returns & Refunds

Hassle-free 7-day replacement on unopened products

Home›Returns & Refunds

We want you to love every Niks Masala product. If something is wrong with your order, we will make it right.

1. What is returnable

  • Damaged or tampered packaging on arrival.
  • Wrong product shipped (different from your order).
  • Manufacturing defect (e.g. moisture damage, foreign body).
  • Quantity short versus the invoice.

2. What is not returnable

For food-safety reasons, we cannot accept returns of opened, used or partially consumed spice packs. We also cannot accept returns of:

  • Products purchased on clearance or marked "final sale".
  • Products requested for return after 7 days of delivery.
  • Products where the original tamper-evident seal has been broken.

3. How to request a return

  1. Email returns@niksmasala.com or WhatsApp +91 73385 19975 within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Include: your order ID, a description of the issue, and 2–3 clear photographs of the product and packaging.
  3. Our team will respond within 24 working hours with a return authorisation and the next steps.
  4. Pack the item in its original packaging. We arrange free reverse pickup in serviceable pincodes.

4. Replacement

Once the returned item is inspected at our Mangalore facility (typically within 3 working days of pickup), we will dispatch a replacement at no extra cost.

5. Refunds

If a replacement is not possible, we will issue a full refund (including shipping fees) to your original payment method:

  • UPI / Cards / Net Banking: 5–7 working days after refund initiation.
  • Cash on Delivery: bank transfer to your account within 7 working days, once you share your account details with us.

6. Cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched by emailing or calling us. For paid orders, the full amount is refunded within 5–7 working days. Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery and it will be treated as a return.

7. Questions?

Reach us on +91 73385 19975 (Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm IST) or email hello@niksmasala.com. For escalations, contact our Grievance Officer (see Privacy Policy).
