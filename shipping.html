3. Dispatch & delivery times

  • Dispatch: 24–48 working hours from order confirmation.
  • Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad): 3–5 business days.
  • Other Tier-1 / Tier-2 cities: 4–7 business days.
  • Remote / NE / hill stations: 7–10 business days.

Delivery times are estimates. Actual delivery depends on the courier partner, local conditions and pincode serviceability. We are not liable for delays caused by courier partners, weather, festivals, strikes or other force-majeure events.

4. Courier partners

We dispatch via Delhivery, Shiprocket, Bluedart and India Post depending on your pincode. Tracking is provided for every shipment via SMS, email and the Order Tracking page.

5. Serviceable pincodes

We currently ship to all PAN-India pincodes serviced by our courier partners. At checkout, if your pincode is non-serviceable we will inform you immediately — please contact us and we will attempt alternate arrangements.

6. International shipping

International shipping is not currently available. Follow @niks.masala on Instagram for updates.

7. Payment methods

All payments are collected online at checkout via Razorpay. We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) and net banking from all major Indian banks. We do not currently offer Cash on Delivery.

8. Order tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking ID via email/SMS. You can also use our order tracking page with your order ID. For tracking issues, email inihaex@gmail.com.

9. Lost or undelivered packages

If a package is marked "undelivered" or returned to origin (RTO) due to incorrect address, unavailability, or refusal, the customer is responsible for re-shipping charges. If a package is lost in transit, we will file a complaint with the courier and replace or refund within 14 working days.

10. Damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged, please refuse delivery if possible. Otherwise, take photographs before opening and email inihaex@gmail.com within 48 hours. See our Returns Policy for next steps.
